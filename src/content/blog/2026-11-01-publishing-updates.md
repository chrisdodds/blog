"Dip Cone" (CNF) was picked up by [Claudine](https://www.claudineliterary.com/). I'll link it when it's up.

**On writing, generally:**

Life has been busy. I've been writing consistently-ish, but haven't finished many things. I've also been a little more focused on music than writing lately. Too many hobbies.

I'm trying to decide if I lay off flash for a while and focus on longer short stories or another book-shaped thing. Mostly, I just write what comes out, but I can steer that energy more than I have been. When I initially began submitting things I flagged flash and short-shorts as a dopamine trap, and I think there's truth to that. That's not to say the form is "easier," just that the feedback loop is faster than longer works.

It's probably a kindness to myself to slow down a little bit too, as I tend to speed run everything. To be honest, I don't even really know what I'm running toward with writing.

**What about the book?**

Not much to share yet. The manuscript is locked in, blurb requests are in flight, and I should have cover candidates any day now. The remainder of little things that get us to "book in hand" will start clicking into place once the cover is finalized. Most of my work in the first half of 2027 will be marketing-related.

I have to figure out the appropriate level of effort and time investment on that front. While I love my baby and want tons of people to read it, I'm also sensitive to ROI, especially for something like a literary novella. The conversation I have planned with my publisher is basically "What are the 3-5 highest leverage things we can do?" and then filter from there. Like a laser, I'll be. Like a laser.
